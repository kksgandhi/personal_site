Many don't like disruptive protest (e.g. blocking highways, taking over spaces). I don't really get it.

 - If someone is irrationally against the cause, they'll find any excuse to justify their irrational dislike. The dislike comes first, it's not caused by the protesters.
 - If someone is for the cause, the protesters might annoy them, but I can't imagine it would _turn them away from the cause_. I can't imagine someone saying "yeah I used to be super environmentalist but then I saw someone blocking traffic and now fuck the rainforest."
 - If someone doesn't have an opinion, then simply getting the news story in front of them, making them aware is super important.
