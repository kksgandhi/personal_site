Two of my favorite philosophical games (The Talos Principle and 1000xResist) are really, _really_ slow with their philosophy. The number of ideas they present are really low.

Weirdly enough, I think they work really well. They drip feed ideas and then they give you the space necessary to wrestle with them. A barrage of ideas has a lot of information, but you don't have time to chew on anything before the next thing comes along.

This idea would never work in a TV show, it'd be far too boring, but with a game you're interacting, and so it keeps you engage while your mind wrestles with these ideas.
