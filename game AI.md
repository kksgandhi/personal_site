Learning objectives:
- Create believable agents, environments, and interactive narratives with the goal of providing a fun and engaging experience to a player. 
- Design efficient and robust AI systems, such as Finite State Automata, Behavior Trees, ML systems, and Pathfinding systems.
- Describe the history of game AI and major advancements in the field.
- Justify the cost-benefits of various approaches.
- Utilize Procedural Content Generation to extend human creativity.
- Predict user behavior using player modeling.


For AI a combination of these free chapters: https://www.gameaipro.com/ and probably Artificial Intelligence for Games by Ian Millington for a hardcover text.
