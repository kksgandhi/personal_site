Create group contracts. What is your role in the group and what work are you going to do? Then check back in with that at the end.

Questions to ask a group before a group project:

 - What is the "What is Computer Science?" topic that your group will be presenting? 
 - Question 2
 - List the names of your groupmates that worked with you in class today. 
 - (please include first and last names!)
 - Question 3
 - How clear do you feel about what you're team's next steps are?
 - Question 4
 - Please briefly explain your response to question 3.
 - Question 5
 - What are (at least) the next three times you are planning to meet to work on your presentation? 
 - Question 6
 - How is your group plannining to communicate (email, texting, communication platform like Whatsapp/Signal/Teams/GChat/Discord)? Be specific and make sure you have exchanged information among all group members. 
 - Question 7
 - What leadership role are you interested in holding for this group project?
